## Summary

- **Bias, coverage and interval length across follow-up are already stored** under both `weight_variance = "known"` and `"estimated"`, at 11 time points (months 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48), for the survival difference, each arm's survival and the RMST difference, plus the log hazard ratio. The Stage 2e report prints only month 18 (difference) and month 36 (RMST); the full profile is in `dev/audit/stage2e/m7_summary.csv` and `dev/audit/oc/oc_summary.csv`.
- **Two regimes are covered with estimated weights: treated ESS ratio about 0.69 and about 0.33–0.39.** With ESS ratio about 0.69, `"estimated"` covers 0.930–0.954 for the difference at months 3–30 and `"known"` 0.955–0.979. With ESS ratio 0.33–0.39 both are below nominal outside months 6–24, and `"estimated"` is the lower of the two at every time point.
- **Coverage falls late in follow-up in every regime and under both options.** At month 48 the difference is covered at 0.900–0.933 (`"estimated"`, ESS ratio 0.69) and 0.808–0.875 (ESS ratio 0.33–0.39). The estimate is biased downward late, and the treated-arm curve carries the loss.
- **Not stored:** a regime with treated ESS ratio near 0.9 in a full two-arm design; the refitting bootstrap at any time point other than month 18 (difference) and month 36 (RMST); time points defined as percentiles of event times. Details and the run projection are in section 3.

## 2. Table

### Conventions

- **Columns.** true = true value; mean est = true + bias; bias and emp SD are over the evaluable replicates and are the same under both options (the point estimate does not depend on the option); SE = mean standard error; cov = coverage of the nominal 95% interval with its Monte Carlo standard error, sqrt(p(1 − p) / n); len = mean interval length; ESS t = treated-arm ESS ratio.
- **Evaluable replicates.** A replicate counts at time t when each arm has a subject with observed time ≥ t. "reps" shows evaluable / total where they differ.
- **boot column.** Coverage of the refitting bootstrap (normal / percentile) · mean length (normal / percentile) [coverage of known, estimated on the same 500 replicates]. In true-weight cells nothing is refitted.
- **What is printed here.** The survival difference, the RMST difference, the log hazard ratio and the bands, for every 2,000-replicate cell and every stored time point. Each arm's survival (S0, S1), the 500-replicate M7 cells and OC cells 8 and 15 (identical to M7 cells 31 and 32) are in the CSV only, in the same columns.
- **Empty cell** = not stored; listed in section 3.
